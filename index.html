Questions

Do I pay anything for the demo?

No. The demo is free, and so is one alternative design. You only pay if you decide to keep the site.

What does ฿299 a month include?

Your website, hosting, HTTPS, backups, updates, fixes, and one small change every month.

What counts as a small change?

New prices, opening hours, a menu update, up to 2 new photos, or a short text change. Bigger work is quoted first, and you decide.

Why a 12-month minimum?

It keeps the setup at only ฿990. Want a shorter minimum? Choose the ฿4,990 setup and the minimum is 3 months.

Who owns the website and the domain?

The domain is always yours. The website is yours once the minimum term is paid, or straight away if you buy it outright.

What if I stop after the minimum?

You keep your site. We hand over the files and help you move it, or keep hosting it for ฿1,490 a year.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer to our business account (details on the invoice), monthly or yearly. You get a receipt for every payment.

How long does it take?

Usually 3–7 days from your OK to go-live, depending on how quickly we get your feedback.

Can I keep my current domain and email?

Yes. We only point your domain to the new site; your email stays where it is.
